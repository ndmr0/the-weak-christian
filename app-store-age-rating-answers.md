## App Nature

The app is a devotional encouragement app with bundled text content, local saved notes, local journal reflections, local prayer requests, and native share-sheet use.

The app does not provide:

- Medical diagnosis
- Mental health treatment
- Emergency support
- Pastoral counseling chat
- User-to-user communication
- Unrestricted browsing
- Purchases or donations

## Care Note

The app includes a care note explaining that devotional notes are for spiritual encouragement and are not a replacement for pastoral, medical, mental health, or emergency care.

## Expected Rating

The app should qualify for a low age rating if the questionnaire is answered truthfully for the current implementation.

Do not mark the app as **Made for Kids** unless the product strategy changes to target children as the primary audience.

## Important Submission Note

If future versions add user-generated public content, messaging, unrestricted web links, donations, purchases, or medical/mental-health features, review these answers before submission.
