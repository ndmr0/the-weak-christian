Daily Notes of Grace Support

Thank you for using Daily Notes of Grace. This app exists to give simple, Christ-centered encouragement from Bible verses.

Common Help

You can update your name from Profile in the app menu. You can also reset seen notes from the app menu or clear saved notes from the profile controls.

If sharing does not open, try closing and reopening the app, then share again from the preview screen.

Privacy

The app stores your name, saved notes, and seen-note history locally on your device. It does not use accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.
