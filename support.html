The Weak Christian Support

Thank you for using The Weak Christian. This app exists to give simple, Christ-centered encouragement from Bible verses.

Common Help

You can update your name from the profile screen. You can also reset seen notes or clear saved notes from the profile controls.

If sharing does not open, try closing and reopening the app, then share again from the preview screen.

Support This Work

If the app blesses you and you would like to help support it, visit https://ko-fi.com/nelsondavid.

Privacy

The app stores your name, saved notes, and seen-note history locally on your device. It does not use accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.
