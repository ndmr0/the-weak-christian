Daily Notes of Grace Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 6, 2026

Overview

Daily Notes of Grace is designed to provide Christ-centered encouragement from Bible verses. The app does not require an account and does not collect personal information from you.

Data Stored On Your Device

If you choose to enter a name, complete check-ins, save notes, write journal reflections, keep prayer requests, or read notes in the app, that information is stored locally on your device so the app can personalize encouragements, show saved notes, and keep the app useful.

This local data is not sent to the developer by the app.

Notifications

If you turn on the optional daily reminder, the app asks your device for notification permission and schedules a local reminder. The app does not upload notification tokens or send reminder data to the developer.

Accounts, Analytics, Ads, And Tracking

Daily Notes of Grace does not use accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking. The app does not sell personal information.

Sharing

When you use the share feature, the app opens your device share sheet with the message you previewed. The destination app you choose may handle that shared text according to its own privacy policy.

Contact And Support

For support, visit the support page.
